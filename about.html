About SISU

We coach young athletes out of Grouse Strength Studio in Hawthorn East. Ben started SISU, and he and Josh own it together.

/ˈsi.su/ · noun · Finnish

The will to keep going when it gets hard, without making a fuss about it.

We coach the physical side in detail and we test it. The bigger job is the athlete: doing the extra work when nobody's asking, putting up with the boring parts, and learning to lose well.

We want kids to care more about how they prepare and how hard they work than about the result on the day.

Founder

Ben Plowman

I've played basketball most of my life and I still coach it most weekends, at Hawthorn Magic in the VJBL and at Balwyn Blazers, where I've been head coach since 2017. SISU started on that sideline. I kept watching talented kids fade late in games, and more often than not the missing piece was strength and fitness.

I keep my coaching calm and methodical. I'll spot what's off in an athlete's movement before they feel it, and I explain the why, to the kids and to their parents.

Parents

Questions

Is SISU OK for beginners?

Yes. We coach athletes who are new to structured training and athletes who've done years of it. Testing in the first session tells us where each one should start.

How often should my child train?

It depends on their age, their week and how much sport they already play. We'll recommend a load that fits around school and the sport they already play, with their long-term development in mind.

What changes should I see?

The tested numbers are the first thing: jump height, jump distance and sprint time. Parents also tell us about these:

  • More confidence at training and in games
  • Better balance and body control
  • Better decisions under pressure
  • Fewer niggles and injuries
  • A healthier attitude to training
What does SISU mean?

It's a Finnish word for inner strength and determination: keeping going when things are hard, even when the odds look against you.
