How it works

  1. 01
    Foundation session, 60 to 90 minutes

    We look at how you move, where you're strong and where you're not, and what your sport asks of you. You leave with your first program.

  2. 02
    Train in the app

    Your program lives in the app, with a video for every exercise and your numbers tracked as you go.

  3. 03
    Progression session every 5 to 7 weeks

    We check your form and how the training's going, then update the program for the next block.

Who it's for

  1. 01

    Athletes 15 and over with their own gym membership.

  2. 02

    Athletes whose week doesn't fit a group session.

  3. 03

    Athletes who want strength work written for their sport.

  4. 04

    Athletes ready to take charge of their own training, with a coach checking in.
