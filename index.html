/ˈsi.su/ · noun · Finnish

The will to keep going when it gets hard, without making a fuss about it.

It's where the name comes from. We test the physical side. Sisu shows up in other ways, like an athlete coming back the week after a bad game and doing the work.

Founder

Ben Plowman

I've played basketball most of my life and I still coach it most weekends, at Hawthorn Magic in the VJBL and at Balwyn Blazers, where I've been head coach since 2017. SISU started on that sideline. I kept watching talented kids fade late in games, and more often than not the missing piece was strength and fitness.

I keep my coaching calm and methodical. I'll spot what's off in an athlete's movement before they feel it, and I explain the why, to the kids and to their parents.
