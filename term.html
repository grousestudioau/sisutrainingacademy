What's included

One fee for the term. You don't pay per session.

  1. 01
    Testing in the first session

    Vertical jump, seated broad jump and a 10 m sprint. If your athlete misses week one, we test them the next week.

  2. 02
    Ten coached sessions

    One a week in your timeslot, with the load building across the term.

  3. 03
    A program built from their numbers

    We write each athlete's program around their test results and what they need to work on.

  4. 04
    SISU Daily

    A new skill in the app every Sunday at 6pm, about ten minutes a day between sessions.

  5. 05
    Re-test in the final week

    The same three tests between 14 and 18 December.

  6. 06
    A progress report

    Their results, what improved, and what to work on next term.

Questions

Who is Term 4 for?

Athletes aged 10 to 18 who play any sport. Loading and exercises are set for growing bodies, so it's safe across that whole range.

Does my athlete need gym experience?

No. The first session is testing, and we build the program from those numbers. New athletes start in Group with everyone else.

What if they miss a session?

They pick up where they left off the next week. Sessions can't be swapped between groups.

Which sports is it for?

Any. We train strength, power, speed and how athletes move, and that carries into every team sport.
